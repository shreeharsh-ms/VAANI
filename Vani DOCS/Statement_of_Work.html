1. OVERVIEW AND OBJECTIVES

1.1 Black Angler Pvt Ltd will design, build, and launch the core digital foundation for Ms. Palak Paresh Gandhi's speech-language pathology and audiology clinic (Vani's Clinic) in Satara, Maharashtra.

1.2 The project encompasses three primary technology streams: a responsive bilingual clinic website (English/Marathi) with 15 service pages, automated WhatsApp appointment notifications & reminders via Meta Cloud API, and a secure web-based digital patient records dashboard.

1.3 Core Success Metrics:

  • Patients can easily request or confirm an appointment online in under 2 minutes.
  • Automated 24-hour and 2-hour WhatsApp reminders actively minimize appointment no-shows.
  • Practitioner and receptionist can securely record, access, and manage structured clinical notes (speech assessments & audiology charts) from day one of clinic operations.

7. ACCEPTANCE PROCEDURE

7.1 Delivery Notice: Black Angler dispatches a staging link or release notice for each milestone deliverable along with an acceptance checklist.

7.2 Review Window: The Client has 5 working days to review and either approve the deliverable or provide a written list of material non-conformities against the Scope Baseline.

8. TECHNICAL APPROACH, SECURITY AND DATA

  • Architecture: Managed responsive web application (HTML5/CSS3/JS), custom CMS backend, official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API integration, secure relational database for patient records.
  • Hosting & Encryption: Hosted on secure cloud servers located within India. HTTPS/SSL encryption in transit (256-bit) and AES encryption at rest for patient databases.
  • Access Control: Role-based access control (RBAC) separating practitioner administrative access from receptionist booking views. Two-factor authentication (2FA) enforced on administrative logins.
  • Data Protection (DPDP Act, 2023): The Client acts as the Data Fiduciary and Black Angler acts as the Data Processor. Black Angler personnel do not view real patient records except for technical support with explicit permission. Security breach notification SLA: within 48 hours (MSA Section 15.5).
  • Sensitive Records Gating: CBT notes module is encrypted and restricted exclusively to the practitioner login.
  • Account Ownership: Domain names, Google accounts, and Meta WhatsApp accounts are registered under the Client's name. Black Angler acts solely as a delegated technical manager (MSA Section 11).
